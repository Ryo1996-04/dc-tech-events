How an event makes the list

  1. Technical content is the main draw: talks, labs, CTFs or build time. Pure networking nights and sales pitches are left out.
  2. It happens in DC, suburban Maryland or Northern Virginia.
  3. The date appears on the organizer's own page, and anyone can sign up.

Before you register

  • Ask about student pricing. Many conferences offer student tickets, scholarships, volunteer slots or a free livestream.
  • Claim workshop seats early. Hands-on labs cap seats far below the size of the talk rooms.
  • Pack a ready laptop. Install the tools named on the agenda the night before, since venue Wi-Fi is often slow.
  • Carry photo ID. Office buildings and campuses such as MITRE check it at the security desk.
  • Plan the trip. Many venues are a short walk from a Metro station; check the WMATA trip planner.

How this list stays current

Each listing is checked by hand against the organizer's page, and the footer shows the date of the last check. Spotted a change or a missing event? Let us know on GitHub.
